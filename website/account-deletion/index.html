Account & Data Deletion

Learn how to delete your account and associated data from Swimmingo.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your Swimmingo account and all associated data in two ways:

1

Delete from the App (Recommended)

Open Swimmingo → Go to Settings → Scroll down and tap "Reset All Progress". This will delete all swim sessions, nutrition data, and goals from your account. To fully remove your profile, delete each swimmer profile in the profile switcher.

2

Request via Email

Send an email to adminswimmingo@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Include your account email and the reason for deletion (optional). We will process your request within 7 business days.

3

Use the Form Below

Fill out the account deletion request form below. Make sure to use the email address associated with your Swimmingo account.

Request Account Deletion

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • All swimmer profiles and their associated data
  • Swim session history (laps, times, strokes)
  • Goal settings and target times
  • Nutrition logs (calories, protein, carbs, fats)
  • Cloud backup data from Firebase Realtime Database
  • Device mapping record

What is NOT Deleted Immediately

  • Local app data on your device — you must uninstall the app or use "Reset All Progress" to clear it
  • Anonymous analytics data (no personally identifiable information)
  • Ad serving logs (anonymized and not linked to your identity)

Data Retention Period

After you submit a deletion request, your data will be fully removed from our servers within 30 days. During this period, we may retain your data to prevent accidental deletion. After 30 days, all data is permanently and irreversibly deleted.

Need Help?

If you have any questions about the account deletion process, please contact us at adminswimmingo@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Introduction

Dream Creators ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Swimmingo mobile application and the swimmingo.web.app website (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Email address and password (for authentication)
  • Profile information: name, age, height, weight, school/club, city/state
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Swimming performance data (lap times, stroke data, goals)
  • Nutrition data (calories, protein, carbs, fats logged by you)

Device Information

We collect a unique device identifier to enforce single-device mapping and prevent unauthorized account sharing. This identifier is not linked to your personal identity outside of our Service.

Usage Data

We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app experience, including feature usage, crash reports, and performance metrics.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To authenticate users and secure accounts
  • To store and display your swimming performance data, goals, and nutrition logs
  • To enable multi-profile support (up to 2 swimmers per account)
  • To send push notifications for training reminders and goal achievements
  • To sync data to our cloud servers for backup and device switching
  • To improve the app based on usage patterns and feedback

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored locally on your device using SQLite and synced to Google Firebase Realtime Database for backup purposes. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission using HTTPS
  • Firebase Authentication for secure login
  • Single-device mapping to prevent unauthorized access
  • Regular security audits

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect the rights and safety of our users
  • With service providers (Firebase) who assist in operating our Service, subject to confidentiality agreements

Third-Party Services

Our Service uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication - For user sign-in and account management
  • Firebase Realtime Database - For cloud data storage and sync
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging - For push notifications
  • Firebase Analytics - For anonymous usage analytics
  • Google AdMob - For displaying advertisements
  • Google Play Services - For Google Sign-In integration

These third parties have their own privacy policies governing the use of your data.

Children's Privacy

Our Service is designed for parents and coaches to track swimmers' progress. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us. We will take steps to remove that information from our servers.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access - Request a copy of your data
  • Correction - Update or correct your profile information
  • Deletion - Delete your account and associated data (see our Account Deletion page)
  • Data Portability - Request your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw Consent - Opt out of non-essential data collection

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at adminswimmingo@gmail.com.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. Local data on your device can be deleted via the "Reset All Progress" option in the app settings or by uninstalling the application.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: adminswimmingo@gmail.com
  • App: Settings → Privacy Policy (in-app link)
